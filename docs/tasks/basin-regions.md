# Basin regions

**Status:** validating
**Branch:** basin-regions
**Worktree:** .worktrees/basin-regions
**Goal:** The 7.5 km Icarus bake has zones that feel different and make sense at the scale of kilometres: a basin shape, mountains round Nose, a black-mud swamp, a desert, a farm belt of fields and canals round Bowl, and Old Orchard, Glass Flats and the Fallen Sun grown by mapgen round a kept centre, size and core, with Glass Flats moved off road into the SE corner. The user judges it from the overview and close-up renders of the bake and by driving it, and uses it as the base land of the vast map. A close-up of any 200 m window names its zone without colour: by what blocks sight, what slows the truck and which way lines run. Useless if: zones differ only in ground colour; a zone is a blob or one noise threshold with no shape that steers routes; mountains are lumps with no valleys or passes; the grown areas are the old stamps moved or scaled; the farm belt is scattered fences with no grid; swamp and desert are paint over unchanged relief; a road into Nose climbs a cliff or a site cannot be reached.
**Mode:** hands-off

## Context

- `REGION` is a 120-unit hand layout of 13 places and 5 roads, spread by `MAP_SCALE` 15.6 to 1872 tiles of 4 m. Spreading moves places apart and adds no structure.
- Relief is fBm noise in `src/sim/elevation.ts` whose widest octave is 160 tiles, about 640 m. The 7.5 km overview reads as one repeated lumpy texture with no ranges, valleys or rim.
- Hand landforms (canyon, dry river, Bowl crater, Fallen Sun basin) keep tile widths, so at 7.5 km they are thin lines.
- Old Orchard, Glass Flats and the Fallen Sun keep the ground baked on a 600-tile map through `src/mapgen/ground-stamps.bin`. Each is about 400 m across.
- Old Orchard's grown part is already generated by `orchardGrowth()` in `src/data/territory.ts`: field cells, groves, yards, tracks and canals inside an outline.
- Only mapgen and `road-grade.ts` import `src/sim/elevation.ts`. The sim reads heights from the baked map file.
- The full bake takes 521 s, and rain erosion takes 426 s of it (`game/tmp/vast/fullbake.log`). Wind takes 18 s.
- `wind()` in `src/mapgen/geology.ts` is Werner's slab model blowing toward the ESE, so sand drifts toward the SE. `dunes()` stamps one straight 14-tile sawtooth.
- `heightFromElevation()` is about 6 times steeper above elevation 0.32 than below, so raising elevation there makes cliffs.
- `buildRoadNetwork()` throws when a territory has no road entry node. `territoryEntries()` returns only road crossings, and the bot, cheats, hunting grounds and directions reduce over that list.
- The Fallen Sun's authored furrow runs from the crater 15 degrees west of south, so the authored ship came in from the SSW. Broken Wing lies NNE of the crater.
- Lore: Icarus was a fertile farming basin with fields, canals and irrigation gates. Rain is rare. Only Bowl's canals carry water today, filled from barrels. Nose stands on a mountain round a ship wreck. Dustwell's water is counted barrel by barrel. The Glass Flats are sand the ship's engines melted on the way down, thickest along the debris trail, every ripple leaning toward the Fallen Sun. The pod field lies at the edge of the Glass Flats in the debris trail.

## Design

The land is built in two scales. A coarse relief solve gives the km shape: rim, range, valleys, passes and drainage. Zones then pick the rules for ground, detail and props. Zone edges come from causes on that land, never from a radius.

Layout, north up as on the Map tab:

```
+------------------------------------------+
| swamp            Scrapjaw     MOUNTAINS  |
|  Dustwell                 canyon  Nose   |
| river in                                 |
| Orchard        FALLEN SUN    Wing        |
|  farm      impact apron                  |
|  belt          descent path              |
|  canals                     DESERT       |
|  along the                  dunes        |
|  dry river                       GLASS   |
| Bowl        river out            FLATS   |
+------------------------------------------+
```

Parts:

1. Uplift map. Authored in layout units: edge uplift that makes the basin rim, with two gaps where the dry river enters in the west and leaves in the south, and the NE range as a long spine strip with saddles in its crest profile. Uplift drops along each region road corridor before the solve, so a saddle exists where a road crosses the range. The dry river and canyon cells are pinned as channels.
2. Relief solve. A stream-power erosion solve, the implicit Braun and Willett scheme, runs on a coarse grid of 16 or 32 m and gives ranges with spur ridges, trunk valleys, saddles and drainage divides. The result is upsampled and added in height units after `heightFromElevation()`, so it never meets the steep gain above 0.32. Today's fBm stays as detail, its amplitude set per zone. The existing rain, slump and wind finish it. The solve is a bake layer cached by seed and input hash, and adds no second full-grid rain pass.
3. Zones. A zone is a data entry with a kind and a polygon or spine in layout units, so it scales with `MAP_SCALE`. Its weight is a signed distance on the coarse grid. Noise warps only its outer band, never near cores or roads. Layers blend zone parameters such as uplift, roughness and frequency into one noise call, never finished heights, which would leave flat moats at the seams. Relief transitions span 0.5 to 1 km. Ground transitions are 100 to 200 m and dithered. Where the land gives a cause, the edge follows it: a range front, a water line, a canal or the end of sand supply.
4. Mountains. NE corner, behind the canyon. The range front is abrupt, with gravel fans at its foot. Nose sits on a shelf. Roads climb trunk valleys to saddles, and road grading follows a smoothed, grade-limited profile. Grade margins are at least 2.5 times the wall height they cut through, never the fixed 15 tiles.
5. Desert. SE, the downwind end of the basin's sand. The zone runs `wind()` coarser with its own sand supply thinning toward the zone edge, so dune ridges come out 25 to 100 tiles apart and 3 to 6 m high, with flat corridors between them. Windward slopes stay drivable, so only slip faces are cliffs. Dunes slow trucks and block sight, so they are slow cover. One star dune pile stands as a landmark. Green Pit sits on the desert edge as an oasis.
6. Swamp. NW, a spring-fed black mud flat round Dustwell's spring. A water table sits a set depth below the smoothed ground and falls away from the spring. Table minus height sorts tiles into pools of dirty water, `blackMud` and scrub islands. Sloughs stretch along the outflow, and one wet channel runs to the dry river. Hummocks smaller than a tile are props, with dead trees and sunk car wrecks. The water is foul, so it does not compete with Dustwell's counted barrels.
7. Farm belt. West, on the gentle land along the dry river from Old Orchard to Bowl. A headgate on each bank upstream feeds one main canal per bank. Each canal is traced along the contour on smoothed heights at a small constant fall and carved with its banks into the heightfield. Laterals run down the fall line on grid lines. The field grid turns to the mean slope, with cells of about 700 m, which is 178 tiles, split into plots. Tracks run on banks and cell edges, and abandoned farmsteads stand at grid corners. Breakage rises with distance from the headgate: silted canals, missing fence runs, roofless houses. Dry canals are barriers with fixed crossings. Only Bowl's canals hold water. The generator generalizes `orchardGrowth()`.
8. Crash zone. The descent path starts over Glass Flats in the SE corner and curves while the ship brakes, so its last leg runs into the authored furrow from the SSW. An along-track coordinate follows that path to the Fallen Sun. Debris lands in an oval along the track: light hull plate and pods uprange near Glass Flats, cross-track spread growing along the path, dense compact pieces past the crater. The glass is a distance-to-path field that narrows and thickens toward the crater, with ripples leaning toward it. The crater's ejecta apron is rubble that falls off with the cube of distance, lopsided downrange and bare uprange. Ash would not last 120 years, so none is drawn. Broken Wing stays where it is, as a section torn off the ship.
9. Territories. Each keeps an authored core, and its zone's generator grows everything round it. The three territory stamps leave `ground-stamps.bin`, and Canyon Bridge keeps its stamp.
   - The Fallen Sun keeps its centre, authored outline, basin, furrow, hull pieces, reactor, caches, dirt roads and spurs. Hazard and hunting grounds read that outline.
   - Old Orchard keeps its centre, army base and old road skeleton. Its outline is the contour of its zone weight, traced by one pure function. Its main road ends are computed onto that outline, as `edgePoint()` does today.
   - Glass Flats keeps its wreck pieces and moves to the SE corner. Its outline is contoured the same way.
10. Off-road Glass Flats. It loses its two approach roads. A territory no road crosses gets one entry: the outline point nearest the road network. `buildRoadNetwork()` stops requiring a road node for it, and directions end at the nearest road point.

Out of scope: more places, new roads, the old-settlement anchoring band, and travel or nav changes beyond the off-road territory.

Compatibility: the map file version rises for `blackMud`. Old saves need no step for a new map. Old spots, survey sites, claim clues, survey pictures and the Sunward Hollow spot are picked again after the rebake. Talk and quest lines that place Glass Flats or the debris trail are checked against the new layout.

TDD: yes for the pure rules: zone distance fields, uplift, the solve on a small grid, corridor saddles, the territory contour, the swamp water table rule, canal tracing and the farm grid. No for the look, which the user judges from renders.

### Invariants

- IV1 — Zone fields and the uplift map are pure functions of seed and position. Every bake layer reads them from one owner.
- IV2 — No region road tile has a cliff under its width, in any zone.
- IV3 — Every town, site gate and territory core is reachable by driving from the player start. A bake that leaves one cut off throws.
- IV4 — Glass Flats lies in the SE quarter, at least a set number of tiles from every road, and its outline touches no road.
- IV5 — A territory outline comes from one pure function, which membership, nav, hazards and the bake all call.
- IV6 — The bake report prints each zone's ground shares and relief signature, and throws on a miss. Ground: desert mostly sand, swamp mostly black mud, dirty water and scrub, farm belt mostly field, track and canal. Relief: mountains a high height range and a cliff share above a set floor, desert ridges at the set spacing, swamp floor below its surroundings, canals falling downhill along their length. Ground share alone never passes a zone.
- IV7 — Every territory has at least one entry, so no consumer of `territoryEntries()` sees an empty list.
- IV8 — New layers keep the full bake within 1.2 times today's 521 s.

### Principles

- PC1 — Zones and the uplift map are one table in `src/data/terrain.ts`. Each kind's numbers live in its own entry, never inline in a layer.
- PC2 — Cores stay authored and everything round them is generated. No new stamp.
- PC3 — Every generated thing has a cause the land shows: rivers and canals run downhill, the swamp sits at a spring, dunes sit downwind, glass follows the descent path, passes sit at saddles.

### Assumptions

- AS1 — The authored cores still pass their throw checks on generated ground, after small nudges noted in their comments.
- AS2 — Lore accepts a foul black-mud swamp at Dustwell's spring. The lore page gets the line once the user agrees.
- AS3 — An upsampled coarse solve, plus detail, still makes cliffs in the range. If it only makes steep hills, IV6 fails and the range front gets a fault step.

### Unknowns

- UK1 — Bake time of the solve and the coarse dune pass at full size.
- UK2 — Exact zone shapes, tuned on renders with the user. Starting sizes on the 7.5 km map: mountains about 2.5 km across the NE corner, desert about 3 km in the SE, Glass Flats about 1.2 km, swamp about 1.8 km, farm belt about 2 by 3.5 km, Old Orchard about 1 km, Fallen Sun apron about 1.5 km, descent path about 3.5 km to the crater.
- UK3 — Which talk, quest and clue lines name directions that the move of Glass Flats or the trail breaks.

## Plan

Approach: build the km-scale relief first and look at it, then add zone content layer by layer, each checked on a fast preview bake and on renders. The preview script `game/tmp/basin/preview.mjs` bakes base relief, caches geology by input hash, runs the remaining layers and writes the overview and close-ups. All paths below are under `game/`.

### PH1 — Zones and uplift data
- 1.1 `src/data/zones.ts` (create)
  - `ZONES: readonly Zone[]` with `Zone = { id: string; kind: ZoneKind; shape: Vec[]; band: number }`, shapes as polygons in layout units through `scalePoint()`. Kinds: `mountains`, `desert`, `swamp`, `farmBelt`, `crash`.
  - `ZONE_RULES: Record<ZoneKind, ZoneRules>` holding each kind's roughness, uplift and ground numbers. `UPLIFT` holds the rim width and height, the river gaps, the range spine, the road corridor width and the solve numbers. `DESCENT_PATH: Vec[]` in layout units, from Glass Flats to the furrow tail.
  - Respects: PC1.
- 1.2 `src/sim/zones.ts` (create)
  - `zoneWeight(kind: ZoneKind, x: number, y: number): number` — signed distance to the zone polygon on a coarse grid, warped by low-frequency noise in the outer band only, smoothstep over the band, 0 near cores and roads. Weights of overlapping zones normalize to at most 1.
  - `zoneGrid()` caches every kind's coarse grid once.
  - Respects: IV1.
- Commit: Zone shapes and weights for the basin regions.

### PH2 — Relief solve
- 2.1 `src/sim/relief-solve.ts` (create)
  - `solveRelief(seed: number, size: number): ReliefGrid` — uplift on a coarse grid of `UPLIFT.cell` tiles: rim uplift toward the map edges with gaps at the dry river's entry and exit, range uplift along the spine with saddles where region roads cross, zone uplift multipliers, uplift cut along road corridors. Then implicit stream-power steps after Braun and Willett with priority-flood pit filling. The dry river and canyon cells are fixed base level. Cached per seed and size.
  - `macroHeight(seed: number, x: number, y: number): number` — bilinear sample of the solve, in height units. Sites and the Fallen Sun core hold the macro height of their centre, with the flatten falloff.
- 2.2 `src/sim/elevation.ts` (modify)
  - `reliefAt()` scales detail noise by the zone roughness. `broadAt()` stays in elevation units.
  - `bakedHeight(seed, x, y)` = `heightFromElevation(...) + macroHeight(...)`, used by `baseLayer()` and `finishLayer()` in `src/mapgen/bake.ts:118-140`.
  - `stamped()` keeps only the Canyon Bridge stamp.
- 2.3 `src/sim/ground-stamps.ts`, `src/mapgen/ground-stamps.ts`, `src/data/region.ts` (modify) — drop the territory stamp sites, `groundFrom` and `groundOutline`.
- 2.4 `src/data/terrain.ts` `MAPGEN.heightScale` 1000 to 400, so heights reach ±82 units, 328 m.
- 2.5 `src/sim/road-grade.ts` — grade margin grows with the wall height it cuts, at least 2.5 times it.
- Respects: IV1, IV2, AS3. Tests: solve on a 32-cell grid drains to its outlet, rim higher than floor, saddle lower than the spine beside it, `macroHeight` flat across a site.
- Commit: Coarse relief solve gives the basin its rim, range and valleys.

## Conclusion

The 7.5 km Icarus map now has regions that differ on the scale of kilometres. A range rings Nose, a black-mud swamp sits at Dustwell's spring, dunes fill the SE desert, and a farm belt of canals and abandoned farms runs along the dry river past Bowl. Old Orchard, Glass Flats and the Fallen Sun keep only their authored cores and grow from the land. Glass Flats sits off road in the SE corner, and the descent path carries debris and glass from it to the Fallen Sun.

- AS1 held with one nudge: the Sunward Hollow spot and gate bearing moved to the 7.5 km map.
- AS2 is unconfirmed: the lore page carries the swamp line, plus lines on the range, farm belt and desert, all awaiting the user's OK.
- AS3 held: the mountains pass the cliff share check without a fault step.
- UK1: the full bake takes about 230 s.
- UK2: zone shapes were tuned on renders, hands-off.
- UK3: no talk or quest line needed a change.

- The built tile codes moved into `src/sim/terrain.ts` beside the ground type codes. The bake import cycle had left them undefined in sets built at module load.
- PH2 2.5 dropped: the road grade margin did not grow with wall height. The road bed blur took its place. A map test now enforces IV2 instead: no region road sample lies on a cliff.
