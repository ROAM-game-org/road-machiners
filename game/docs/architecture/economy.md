# Economy, jobs and progression

## Economy

- `shopMoney()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` is the one shop writer of truck money, and raises a `shopMoney` event with its kind. Payments between trucks raise `transfer`, `towDone`, `escortPaid`, `guardPaid` or `patch`, and the recorder's `npcLedger()` throws on any NPC money change those events do not explain.
- Bounty money moves through `transfer` and `shopMoney` events of the kinds `bountyFund` (the faction bounty fund that pays a collecting Marshall), `bountyCoffers` (restitution to a faction and the settlement price), `bountyOffice` (the Marshall office's cut at the desk) and `bountyEscrow` (an independent issuer's stake, in and out). `src/sim/crime.ts` makes every one of them.
- `src/sim/duty.ts` owns duty pay: which goals carry a `leg`, what a leg pays (`dutyPay()`), and who pays it. Lawmen get patrol pay from their town, couriers dispatch pay from the shop they reach, convoy guards pay from their convoy, and `escortFee()` prices a merc's trip there and back with the same formula. `guardFee()` in `src/sim/hire.ts` prices a day of a hired guard with it, a day's turns at `EFFORT.refSpeed`.
- `src/sim/resources.ts` accesses driver resources. NPC fuel and supplies use the player base rules.
- `src/sim/economy.ts` owns paid transactions and part prices, and the scrap patch that gets a broke, stranded or low-on-fuel player moving again at a town. For a truck with no engine and nothing to pay with, the scrap patch also fits the cheapest shelf engine on credit (`partCredit` money, `engineCredit` event). `scrapFuel` is the shared top-up to 40% of the tank that NPC service also uses for a broke driver at a serving site.
- `src/sim/salvage.ts` owns site and wreck stock shared by all collectors, and its daily renewal. A rolled stock keeps its loot in `hidden`, and its other fields are the revealed loot. `revealTurn()` moves hidden units to revealed on `world.searchRng`, a separate stream, and restock adds to `hidden` only. Piles and truck wrecks start with an empty `hidden`. It also owns looting a knocked-out NPC truck, where installed parts come off in a refit job whose pickup names the truck. Sites slowly restock, and so do the loot spots of a territory: each baked spot prop gets a stock with its own id and reach at world creation (`isLootSpot`, `spotTable` in `src/sim/territory.ts`) (by the site rule). Looted road wrecks are replaced beyond the player's gray vision. `salvagePlace()` says what a stock is: a pile, a site, a wreck (a road wreck, or a spot whose look is in `WRECK_LOOKS` in `src/data/territory.ts`) or a plain spot. `WRECK_LOOKS` holds the army truck, the Fallen Sun's hull and ship caches, Glass Flats' engine cache and its dead truck, and an old-world tank hulk, and a compound or Orchard building is a plain spot. The HUD reads it for its prompts, so a plain spot reads Search and Loot and a wreck reads Search the wreck and Loot the wreck.
- `src/sim/salvage.ts` also owns old-world places and their loot spot picks. It groups the baked old-world buildings, tank hulks and riverbed boats into places by spacing and picks at most one off-road, reachable prop per place by a hash of the map seed. Making the picks builds a nav layer of the baked props, which takes seconds, so `npm run old-spots` writes them to `src/data/old-spots.json` and a test fails when that file is stale. Run it after a map rebake or a change to `OLD_PLACES`. A pick's stock id is `old-<type>-<propId>`, so the id holds the place type, and `oldSpotOf()` reads it. `stockOldSpots()` in `salvage.ts` rolls every pick's stock from `OLD_TABLES` at world creation and at load, and `renewSalvage()` restocks them like territory spots. An old spot stock never leaves the world. A table's `rare` pool gives a share of its spare parts as better car parts with low wear.
- `tripFuelCost()` in `src/sim/npc-decisions.ts` owns what the fuel for an NPC trip costs: tiles times the chassis fuel per tile times the fuel supply price. `runOffers()` drops a trade run whose load, sized by `affordableBuyCount()` as `resolveTrade()` buys it, does not earn more than that. The income harness's hauling margin index uses it too.
- Dustwell and Green Pit are stalls like the Granary: they sell fuel and supplies at the flat prices, make water and stock a few parts. Nothing refills supplies for free, for the player or an NPC, except the rationed reserve below.
- `SITE_ECONOMY` in `src/data/market.ts` is the production graph: each site's recipes, demand and service draw. `SHOPS` derive their `makes`, `needs` and `goods` from it. `graphEdges()` lists every made good with each site that uses it.
- `ShopState.goods` in `src/sim/market.ts` holds each shop's crates. `moveStock()` is the one writer, with a named cause: trade, recipe, demand, upkeep, repair, service, contract or relief. `pressureOf()` derives a price's pressure from stock and `stockTarget()`, so pressure is never saved. `watchStock()` lets a harness or a test tally every change by cause.
- `src/sim/production.ts` owns the production clock: recipes, demand, upkeep, site events on `world.marketRng`, and the relief trickle for essential goods. `advanceProduction()` runs in the turn pipeline after `advanceShops()`.
- `topUp()` and `buySupply()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` draw service units from the shop's drums or rations (`SERVICE_GOOD`), keeping an opened crate in `ShopState.opened`. `serviceRoom()` caps a sale at the `scrapFuel()` share of the tank or stores while `rationing()`. Camps keep no stock and sell as before.
- The market tab shows stock, prices, the Rationing tag and road news. Recipes, where a good is made and used, and the slow import show only under the `seeall` console view, which `src/ui/see-all.ts` keeps outside the world. `shortHere()` in `src/sim/market.ts` owns the short rule that the Short here tone and the quest query `short_of()` share.
- `src/sim/road-news.ts` owns road news: a lost shipment in `world.roadNews`, written by `yieldTo()`, `abandonSpill()`, `knockOutNpc()` and `wreckVehicle()`, and `legRisk()`, which `runOffers()` and `supplyOffers()` in `npc-decisions.ts` read through a map cached on the turn and the entries.
- A shipment is the `shipment` on an NPC's sell leg, set by `resolveTrade()`. Convoys no longer load free cargo: the `haul` idle choice builds a trade run from `supplyOffers()`.
- NPCs trade, sell and fuel at stalls as well as town garages, and stalls fuel and repair them like garages, except raiders, who sell cargo and get repairs only at their camps. A camp is a fence paying `campGoodPrice()`, with no shop state.
- `src/sim/escort-job.ts` owns the escort contract: the board offer a waiting trader posts, the danger gate (`escortFit()` reads `fightOdds()`), `routeThreat()`, the abandonment and stuck clocks, the hold order, the outcomes and the share. The shop pays it through `finishContract()` in `market.ts`. The player is the holder of an ordinary `escort` state from `tow.ts`.
- `src/sim/market.ts` owns shops: goods stock and the prices it gives, finite part stock, restocks and contract boards. A shop's `staples` are parts every roll holds, which keeps a stock engine on the Bowl and Nose shelves. A haul contract rolls from `haulLegs()`, graph edges whose destination is below target, and loads from and delivers into stock. A haul's travel pay reads `EFFORT.wage[1]` for every good, and the good's value pays only through the commission. A search contract (kind `search`) targets one driver with a fresh `prices` memory of the posting shop and holds a lead the radio topic `lastSeen` moves through `noteSearchLead()`. `takeContract()` loads its parcel, a `GridItem` of kind `parcel` that names the contract, and `finishContract()` removes it when the search fails or lapses. `searchRefusal()` refuses a second search or a truck with no free cell for the parcel. `advanceContracts()` throws when a held, undelivered search has no parcel aboard or a parcel has no open search. `src/sim/parcel.ts` owns the handover: the topic `parcel` adds a `handover` state and a `meet` goal like a trade, `readyHandover()` is the pure query the action bar reads, and `handOverParcel()` is the only writer of `delivered`. The search then pays like a bounty at the posting shop. Nobody else takes a parcel: `looseCargo()`, `isLoot()` and the pile code skip or refuse it, a chassis swap keeps its id, and the dump command refuses it. Its rolls draw from `world.marketRng`, a separate stream, so shop changes never shift combat or NPC randomness. `src/sim/rescue.ts` owns rescue contracts: who may be named (`isRescueCandidate()`), the roll, the offer check that drops an offer the turn its driver stops needing help, the endings in `advanceRescues()` right after `advanceStates()`, and `namedClients()`, which the watchdog reads so a posted driver waits. `src/sim/survey.ts` owns survey sites with their props, lay and fair clues, the survey roll, the clue a shop tells (`surveyClue()`, the one place a future landmark system plugs in), the find by sight (`findSurveySites()`, a turn pipeline call after vision, never run on load), the survey job and what a finished site records. Its rolls draw from `world.marketRng`, a separate stream, so shop changes never shift combat or NPC randomness. `src/data/market.ts` holds shop profiles, the effort model and contract terms.
- `finishContract()` in `src/sim/market.ts` is the one end of a held contract. It works out the money of the ending once (the reward, an escort's share or a failed haul's penalty), pays or charges it, and appends a `PastContract` with a deep copy of the contract, the outcome, the turn and that amount to `player.pastContracts`, keeping the newest `CONTRACTS.pastKept`. The journal reads that list. `src/ui/contract-row.ts` draws the held contract row that the town Contracts tab and the journal share.
- Money is whole cents in the sim and data, 100 to an M (`UNITS.centsPerM`). `moneyAmount()` and `moneyText()` in `src/ui/units.ts` are the one money display. They show whole M, rounded away from zero, and the HUD's `moneyLabel()` builds on them.
- `src/sim/claims.ts` owns salvage claim contracts: which remote wreck stocks and parts may be named, the reward, the window and the rival's send turn, the clue (`claimClue`) and its candidate wrecks (`claimCandidates`), the choice and send of a rival crew (`sendRival`), and where the claimed part is (`claimPartHeld`: in its wreck, with the player, with the rival, or gone). The reservation is derived, never stored: `claimedPartIn()` in `src/sim/salvage.ts` reads the claim contracts on the boards and in the player's hands, and `collectSalvage` and `canTakeAny` keep every truck but the player's and the claim's rival off the named part. `revealClaimed()` in `salvage.ts` turns the part up when a search of its wreck finishes, for every truck. `src/sim/claim-run.ts` runs the rival's `claim` goal: candidate by candidate, search, take, then hand in. `market.ts` settles both hand-ins, `handInClaim` for the player and `handInClaimBy` for the rival, which pays through `shopMoney` and lapses a held claim with the cause `rival`. A claim never creates its part, and a lost part lapses a held claim with no penalty. The radio lead is the `claimWreck` topic in `dialogue-rules.ts`, and the leads live on the held claim.
- `atGarage()` and `requireGarage()` in `src/sim/garage.ts` gate the player's garage work: a Roaming shop from `shopAt()`, or a reached Chaos Highway outpost. Every garage keeps garage storage and makes part moves instant, and away from one a part move is a refit job. Trade, per-part repair and contracts still read `shopAt()`, and town chassis sales and rebuilds `townAt()`.
- `receiveBoughtPart()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` puts every bought part on the truck if it fits and in storage if not, for town and outpost buys alike.
- `garageChassis()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` owns which chassis a town garage sells, from `TOWN_CHASSIS` in `src/data/chassis.ts`, and throws on a town with no lineup. `buyChassis()`, the trucks tab and the bot's chassis orders read it, and `buyChassis()` throws on a chassis the town does not sell. `PLAYER_CHASSIS` stays the list of chassis a player can drive.
- A loot table's `relicChance` is the chance a rolled stock holds one hidden ship relic. `restockSite()` adds one at `relicChance` times `SALVAGE.restockShare` to a stock with none. Only `hullCache` and `hullScrap` have a chance above 0, and a good is priced only at shops that list it in `goods`.
- `repaint()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` sells a paint job at a town garage (`requireTown()`). It pays `paintPrice()`, `PAINT.share` of the current chassis value, and sets `player.paint`, a `TruckPaint` of three `PALETTE` ids in `src/data/chassis.ts`: `body`, `cab` and `gear`. It throws on an unknown id and on the current paint. Only it and the rescue read the paint in the sim, and render reads it for the player's trucks. The Paint tab of a town garage sells it, and the Trucks tab has no paint control. The tab's scheme row plans one of `PAINT_SCHEMES` whole, a ready-made body, cab and gear set, and Buy sends that plan to the same `repaint()`. Schemes are not saved. The data test keeps every palette color muted: only earth hues may be saturated, a pale color is sand-like, every color is at least `PAINT.minApart` from each role body, and a scheme's body and cab are at least `PAINT.schemeContrast` apart.
- `swapChassis()` in `src/sim/economy.ts` is the one chassis swap: price, built-in parts, refitting the old parts, goods and stores. `buyChassis()` calls it in a town and `outpostBuyChassis()` at an outpost. Goods that would not fit refuse it with `noCargoRoom`, and `chassisSwapRefusal()` runs it on a clone to give the Swap button its reason.
- `src/sim/outposts.ts` owns the Chaos Highway outpost commands. They need the player parked in the yard of the paid next outpost, reuse the garage repair, supply and part prices from `economy.ts`, and sell parts for field patching at `FURY_ROAD.goodsMarkup`. `src/sim/fury-road.ts` pays each outpost once as the `outpostPay` money source. See [Chaos Highway](../wiki/mechanics/fury-road.md).
- `src/sim/wear.ts` owns part condition: max HP, worn stats, wear steps and junk. Only it writes part HP, and a test enforces that.

## Jobs

`src/sim/jobs.ts` owns parked multi-turn jobs, which moving cancels. Refits, field repair in `src/sim/repair.ts` and searches in `src/sim/search.ts` are jobs. Each search turn reveals loot, with a higher chance for a working scraper's knife. `workTimeMult()` in `src/sim/utility.ts` scales refit and roadside patch time by the patcher crane, and `refitTurns()` in `src/sim/inventory.ts` and `patchPlan()` in `src/sim/patch.ts` read it. `src/sim/inventory.ts` holds the inventory commands, and `src/sim/grid.ts` answers grid queries. `workOf()` in `src/sim/states.ts` is the one source for timed work a truck does. That is its job or a state whose kind declares work, like a patch. The HUD and the markers over NPCs read only it, so new timed work gets a progress bar.

## Sites

`src/sim/sites.ts` owns site gates and pads. Trucks never enter a site. Gates lie where roads cross the site edge. Small locations keep one gate, and towns and large locations keep one per road. Each gate has a pad outside it. Services, salvage, NPC visits and site clicks all use pads.

## Progression

`src/sim/progress.ts` owns XP, skill ranks and perks. `practice()` is the only way to gain XP, and `xpFor()` is its pure rule. XP goes into the shared pool `player.xp`, and each source's skill names the activity family whose daily cap it counts against. `buyRank()` is the only way to raise a rank, and `skillEffect()` and perk prerequisites read `player.ranks`. `hasBasic()` is the one test of a rank 1 basic technique (`BASICS` in `src/data/skills.ts`), derived from the ranks and never saved: `overdriveBlock()`, `douseBlock()` (which reads the Overdrive basic), `firstAidBlock()`, `knowsMarket()`, the `playerPatches` condition and `canLookOver()` read it. A start kit's `ranks` sets every rank of a new world. Every practice event names a target, and repeats on one target pay less by the source's `repeat` factor. A new XP hook needs a target that a player cannot renew for free. `skillEffect()` and `vehicleHasPerk()` return nothing for any truck but the player's. XP sources, costs, caps, effects and perks live in `src/data/skills.ts`. `src/sim/progression/` holds the bots, the recorder and the replay behind the `progression:` commands, and the income harness behind `npm run income`, which plays robber, hauler and scavenger policies on the `midgame` kit and reconciles every money change. The recorder bots spend the pool with `buyCheapestRanks()`, so their runs play with the effects their XP pays for. Replay reuses `xpFor()`, so tuning XP numbers needs no new recording.
