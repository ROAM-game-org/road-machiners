Targeting computer, interactive mockup

Click a gun card to select it. All selects every gun, and a second press clears the selection. A second click on a selected card clears it too. Reload and Hold act on the selected guns. Reload on a harpoon cuts its line, and so do the aim buttons below. End turn plays a turn: aimed guns fire, empty guns reload, and the utilities count down.
