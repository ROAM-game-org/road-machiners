# Chaos Highway story game

The evidence for [Chaos Highway](chaos-highway.md): a story game run for issue 703 with seven separate players on the small model and 65 Fate rolls from a recorded seed. It follows the [Story game](story-game.md) rules in two passes. Pass A, the Magnus years, ran four 6-year turns. Pass B, the run, played the hiring and the ends of stretches 1, 4, 8 and 12.

# Setup


Game master: the design agent. Players: separate subagents on the small model (haiku), each started once and continued every turn.
Rules: docs/lore/story-game.md, with the smaller-pass rules. Dice: 4dF rolled by python with a recorded seed (see log.md).

### Place and time

- An unnamed region far from Icarus, in the late Chaos. Nobody here has heard of a fallen ship. Years count as "years before the run" (Y-28 ... Y0). The run of the game starts at Y0.
- An Old World highway, four lanes wide, runs north through badlands for longer than anyone has driven. Its verges hold war leftovers: pileups, army checkpoints, tank lines, rockfalls. Walled forts stand along it at intervals.
- The link exists and armies still want it. Late in the Chaos a few workshops can still fit link contacts.

### Scale

- Pass A, the Magnus years: 4 turns of 6 years, Y-28 to Y-6. Players: Magnus's gang, the fort keepers, a northern settlement, a southern power.
- Pass B, the run: turn 0 is the hiring at Y0, then turns at the end of stretches 1, 4, 8 and 12. Players: Ala, Geezer, the fort keepers, Ala's backers, the bounty setters. Pass A's results are fixed events in pass B.

### Anchors (from the member's request, fixed)

- A1 Magnus led a large raider gang, never outmatched in cruelty or efficiency.
- A2 The gang dispersed. Every one of his fellows is dead. Magnus vanished and lives as Geezer, who says only that he once rode with Magnus.
- A3 At Y0 Geezer is in his fifties, a linked driver with deep link and bullet scars, a daredevil who takes the hardest jobs, said to have killed many. He does not talk about it. He sees all he did as vanity and takes suicide jobs to be killed.
- A4 Shortly before Y0 a truck blew up under him (a setback he admits to).
- A5 Ala, a linked driver of a mammoth 18-wheeler, goes north. There is a bounty on her truck and many will come for it. She pays per stretch and wants no questions. Nobody understands why anyone pays to go north.
- A6 The barman of an outpost sells Geezer's services to Ala. She doubts him for his small truck; he shows his scars and admits the setback; he signs up.
- A7 End of stretch 1: Ala says he is tougher than he looks. End of stretch 4: she asks how he is such a death machine; he reveals a bit; he can ask about the journey. End of stretch 8: she asks why he does it; he asks why so many hunt the truck and why she does not leave it; she reveals more of her mission.
- A8 Later Geezer learns Ala's purpose and keeps going to redeem himself a little. The run never ends, so no ending is imposed.

### Questions play must answer

- Q1 Who keeps the forts?
- Q2 Who is behind Ala, what is in the north and why must she go there?
- Q3 Who opposes her and puts the bounties on the truck?
- Q4 What was Magnus's gang and how did it end?

# Game master truths


- T1 (anchor) Magnus survives the end of his gang and lives on as Geezer. Nobody alive knows.
- T2 (decided before turn A1) The abandoned army column at the tank line was a link regiment. Its trucks' seats still work, but each seat is wired to one driver's nerves and burns the next driver who takes it. Magnus's deep link scars come from forcing a seat that was not his.
- T3 (decided at A4) The burned body brought in as Magnus was not him. He tore the plug out of the burning seat (the deep ring scar) and walked into the badlands, then drove for hire as Geezer.
- T4 (decided at A4) The exile who sold the body died two years later collecting another bounty. No Burnt Driver who knew Magnus's face is alive.
- T5 (decided at B0, from the crews player's private answer) The trailer holds sealed fuel casks, pump and valve assemblies and the crews' well books, which show Blackwell's last well dies within months. Ala is Rook Halloran's daughter.
- T6 (B3) After Hedda called the bounty off, the hunters kept coming on their own account.

- The story game compressed the road so that Northgate's gate came after the twelfth stretch. ### Answers
- Q1: the Keepers hold the forts. They took the empty army forts when the army withdrew and toll every truck. They count every truck through the gate. Commandant Orsolya Dane, 72, has led them for forty years. They are tied to Northgate by the north road pact.

The crews are the children of workers Blackwell bought for fuel, and they owe Blackwell everything they own. North is Northgate, a shut valley with a pure mountain spring, whose water has gone south in Blackwell tankers on Blackwell's terms for twenty years. Ala carries the books and parts as proof that the crews are worth taking in before Blackwell dies. Hedda executed Rook for it, and Ala drives on for the two hundred people who follow.
- Q3: Oilmistress Hedda Vell of Blackwell put the bounty on the truck, to keep the well books from Northgate and the crews in Blackwell's debt. She called it off after the eighth stretch, but the hunters kept coming on their own account: hired guns left in the badlands, and every driver who heard the bar talk that the trailer carries Blackwell's secret. The bounty outlived its payer.
- Q4: see pass A. Magnus led the Burnt Drivers. They took the linked war trucks of a dead army at a tank line, grew to thirty trucks on fear and Northgate's stolen water, and died in one winter, starved between the forts and hunted down for Blackwell's diesel bounty. He tore the plug out of his burning seat and walked away. A burned body was sold as his.
