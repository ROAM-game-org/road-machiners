# Chaos Highway

The world of the Chaos Highway run, as the story game wrote it ([the log](chaos-highway-story-game.md)). It is a separate region in the late Chaos, far from Icarus. Nobody here has heard of a fallen ship, and nothing from the Icarus pages leaks into it: no Icarus place, group or year. Years count as "years ago", never "since the fall".

The first part is what people on the road could know. [Hidden truths](#hidden-truths) holds the answers, which the scenes show as evidence and state only where they say.

## The road

- An Old World highway, four lanes wide, runs north through badlands for longer than anyone has driven. War leftovers line it: pileups, army checkpoints, tank lines and rockfalls.
- Walled forts stand along it at intervals. They were army forts. The army withdrew, and the Keepers took them.
- The link exists and armies still want it. A few workshops can still fit link contacts, and a linked driver is a rare and valuable thing.
- A stretch is the road between two forts. A fort is where a truck is counted, tolled, fuelled and repaired. The run has no last fort that anyone has counted.

## The Keepers

- They keep the forts. Commandant Orsolya Dane took the empty army forts when the army withdrew and made them toll posts. She has led them for forty years and is 72 at the run.
- They toll every truck and count every truck through the gate. Dane's habit is to count.
- The Keepers sit between Blackwell and Northgate. The north road pact ties them to Northgate.
- They know the army's count of the war trucks, which is how they know what the Burnt Drivers took. After the eighth stretch they refuse Blackwell's bounty at their forts, and they shut the road to Blackwell's tankers while the hunting goes on.

## The Burnt Drivers

- Magnus's gang. Magnus took the linked war trucks of a dead army at a tank line, two days north of Blackwell. The seats of some were wired to the drivers who died in them and burned whoever forced them, so the gang took its name from the burns.
- They killed the Keepers' three drivers at the tank line, took the first Blackwell water convoy at a pass with no survivors, and grew to thirty trucks on fear and stolen water.
- One winter caught the gang between the third and fifth forts. No fort fed it, and Blackwell put a diesel bounty on every head, on Magnus's most. The gang starved and split by spring, and young drivers turned on each other for the bounty. Every Burnt Driver was hunted down.
- The forts' story is that Magnus burned in his seat at the tank line, and a burned body was brought in and paid for. Nobody under thirty believes half of the tale of Magnus.
- Old Burnt Driver bounty notices still hang behind some fort bars.

## Blackwell

- The refinery town at the road's south end. Every truck on the road burns its diesel, and Blackwell pays in fuel, never in coin. It buys whoever is for sale.
- Its wells are failing. The covenant, its water deal with Northgate, has run every season for twenty years on Blackwell's terms.
- Oilmaster Kasimir Vell ran it through the Magnus years. He died in the gap, and his daughter Hedda Vell, the Oilmistress, 41 at the run, runs it now. She never lets a debt go unpaid, in either direction.
- The well crews work its wells. They are the children of workers Blackwell bought for fuel, and they owe it everything they own.

## Northgate

- A shut valley with a pure mountain spring at the road's north end. Few southerners have reached it, its gates shut to anyone who comes up the road, and its winters kill the careless.
- Since twenty years ago its water has gone south in Blackwell tankers under the covenant. Northgate failed the bargain and gave too much at the gate.
- Warden Lio Marten ran it until he died in the eighth stretch's week. Wren Sallow, 46, Blackwell-born, the clerk who stayed, runs it now. She counts every barrel twice.
- The game keeps Northgate beyond the last fort anyone has counted. The hauler never arrives, and no scene shows the gate.

## People of the run

- Ala, 34, drives the mammoth hauler. She pays by the stretch and takes no questions. She counts the money out loud before she pays. Drive +3, Will +2.
- Geezer, 54, is the linked driver of a small scout, hired in a fort bar. He has deep link scars at the neck and old bullet scars, and he says only that he once rode with Magnus. He checks every gun pin before he speaks. He took the hardest jobs for two years.
- Tibor, the barman of the Keepers' southern outpost, sells men like parts.
- Mina Ostrander, 39, Rook's second and a pump fitter, leads the crews after Rook. She says the names of the dead aloud.
- Rook Halloran, 58, foreman of Blackwell's well crews. He packed the trailer. He is dead from the fourth stretch's week.

## The run

- Ala drives the hauler north and pays Geezer by the stretch. In the game, Geezer is the player's gun truck, and the hauler is the player's second truck. The one wallet holds the pay, and Geezer keeps both trucks fitted out of it. Ala asks no question about the money.
- A bounty rides on her truck. Hedda set it in diesel, paid at Blackwell: the trailer brought back sealed, the driver alive or dead. Every scavenger, merc and out-of-work bounty man on the road heard it. That is why the groups never stop.
- After the eighth stretch Hedda calls the bounty off. The hunters keep coming on their own account: guns left in the badlands who are owed diesel, and every driver who heard the bar talk that the trailer carries Blackwell's secret. The bounty outlived its payer.
- By the twelfth stretch two hundred crew people and families, in old trucks with no escort, are on the road a day behind the hauler.

## Hidden truths

Spoiler note: scenes before the twelfth stretch must not state T1.

- T1. Magnus lives as Geezer. Nobody alive knows. Ala learns by the twelfth stretch only that he was a Burnt Driver, and that he tore a plug out of a burning seat. The player sees the name in the twelfth stretch flashback and may confess it.
- T2. The abandoned army column at the tank line was a link regiment. Each seat is wired to one driver's nerves and burns the next driver who takes it. Magnus's worst neck scars come from forcing a seat that was not his.
- T3. The burned body brought in as Magnus was not him. He tore the plug out of the burning seat, which left the deep ring scar at the base of his skull, and walked into the badlands. He drove other people's trucks for hire as Geezer and never went back for anyone.
- T4. The exile Magnus sent away for talking of the bounty led Blackwell's hunters to the tank line and sold the body. He was killed two years later collecting another bounty, and with him the last Burnt Driver who knew Magnus's face died.
- T5. Ala's trailer holds sealed fuel casks, pump and valve assemblies and the crews' well books, which show that Blackwell's last well dies within months. It is the crews' proof to Northgate that they are worth taking in. Ala is Rook Halloran's daughter, the only one he taught to drive the link.
- T6. Hedda had Rook executed in the refinery courtyard in front of the crews, when she feared the town walking away. She called the bounty off after the eighth stretch.

Answers the play gave:

- Who keeps the forts: the Keepers, under Dane.
- Who is behind Ala, and what is in the north: Rook and the crews, and Northgate's spring, for the crews' sake.
- Who opposes her: Hedda Vell of Blackwell, to keep the books from Northgate and the crews in debt.
- Magnus's gang: the Burnt Drivers, and how they ended.

Game master adaptations:

- The story game put Northgate's gate after the twelfth stretch. The run is endless, so canon keeps Northgate beyond the last fort anyone has counted.
- Ala pays Geezer by the stretch and he keeps both trucks fitted from that pay, which matches the one wallet.
