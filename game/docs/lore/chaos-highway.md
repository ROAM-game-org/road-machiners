# Chaos Highway

The skeleton of the Chaos Highway story. Everything else is blank and will be filled later.

The run is set in the late Chaos, in an unknown place that has nothing to do with Icarus. The Icarus pages never leak into it.

## Ala

- Ala is a linked driver of a mammoth 18-wheeler, going north-east. She needs protection and pays per stretch of the great highway.
- Nobody understands why she wants to go north-east and pay for it. She asks no questions of her hire and wants none asked.
- Ala's look: black hair in a raw cut, a slight frown, an oversized tanker jacket, and two distinct dark moles, one below the right eye and one above the right lip. Concept art: [ala-concept.png](../concepts/ala-concept.png).
- Ala is an experienced linked driver. Nobody green drives a mammoth hauler, so every version of her story keeps this.
- A bounty rides on her truck, and many will come after it. She tells her hire so at the start.
- Ala does not set out on a suicide mission. The first stretches bring only a few attackers and the chase grows stretch by stretch. She expects trouble, never this much, and every version of her story keeps this too.
- Her mission, who is behind her, what waits in the north-east and who set the bounty are all blank.

## Geezer

- Geezer is the player. He is in his fifties, a linked driver with deep link scars and bullet scars, and he drives a small scout. His truck is not intimidating.
- He is an excellent daredevil driver who takes the hardest jobs, and people say he has killed many men.
- He does not like to talk about it, but long ago he rode with Magnus. Magnus was a bloodthirsty raider, never outmatched in cruelty or efficiency, who led a large gang until it dispersed.
- The deeper secret is that Geezer is Magnus, or was, a long time ago. He grew to see all he did as vanity, and all his fellows are dead. He is a guilt-stricken shell who takes suicide jobs to be killed.
- Recently a truck blew up under him, and he should have died. He is sore that he did not.
- His arc is redemption. He keeps going at first because he has found the ride that kills him. Later he learns Ala's purpose and goes on to redeem himself a little by helping her.
- The gang's name and how Magnus disappeared are blank.

## The hiring

- Tibor, the barman of an outpost, sells Geezer's services to Ala.
- Ala doubts Geezer because his truck is not intimidating. He shows her the link scars and the bullet scars and admits his recent setback. He signs up.
- At the end of stretch 1, Ala says Geezer is tougher than he looks.
- At the end of stretch 4, she asks how he is such a death machine. The player reveals a little and may ask her a little about the journey.
- By stretch 8, Ala seriously asks why he does it. Geezer asks why so many hunt the truck and why she will not run away from it. She reveals more of her mission.
- The run is endless. No ending is imposed.
