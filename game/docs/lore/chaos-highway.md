# Chaos Highway

The world of the Chaos Highway run, as two story game runs wrote it ([the log](chaos-highway-story-game.md)). It is a separate region in the late Chaos, far from Icarus. Nobody here has heard of a fallen ship, and nothing from the Icarus pages leaks into it: no Icarus place, group or year. Years count as "years ago", never "since the fall".

The first part is what people on the road could know. [Hidden truths](#hidden-truths) holds the answers, which the scenes show as evidence and state only where they say.

## The road

- An Old World highway, four lanes wide, runs north through badlands for longer than anyone has driven. War leftovers line it: pileups, army checkpoints, tank lines and rockfalls.
- Walled forts stand along it at intervals. They were army forts. The army withdrew, and the Keepers took them.
- The link exists and armies still want it. A few workshops can still fit link contacts, and a linked driver is a rare and valuable thing.
- A stretch is the road between two forts. A fort is where a truck is counted, tolled, fuelled and repaired. The run has no last fort that anyone has counted.
- Forts pass news to each other by relay slips. A slip is short, and it reaches every fort within days.

## The Keepers

- They keep the forts. Commandant Orsolya Dane took the empty army forts when the army withdrew and made them toll posts. She has led them for forty years and is 72 at the run.
- They toll every truck and count every truck through the gate. Dane's habit is to count, and she keeps ledgers of all of it.
- The Keepers sit between Blackwell and Northgate. The north road pact ties them to Northgate.
- They know the army's count of the war trucks, which is how they know what the Burnt Drivers took.
- Walkers and families with no papers are turned back at the forts. In the run's third week a boy dies on the verge outside the southern fort, and the Keepers let the walking sick through, toll-free, one fort at a time.
- Bram is an old gatekeeper who has counted trucks for thirty years and keeps the ledger of the starving winter.

## The Burnt Drivers

- Magnus's gang. Magnus took the linked war trucks of a dead army at a tank line, two days north of Blackwell. The seats of some were wired to the drivers who died in them and burned whoever forced them, so the gang took its name from the burns.
- They killed the Keepers' three drivers at the tank line, took Blackwell's first water convoy at a pass with no survivors, and grew to thirty trucks on fear and stolen water.
- One winter caught the gang between the third and fifth forts. No fort fed it, and Blackwell put a diesel bounty on every head, on Magnus's most. The gang starved and split by spring, and young drivers turned on each other for the bounty. Every Burnt Driver was hunted down.
- The forts' story is that Magnus burned in his seat at the tank line, and a burned body was brought in and paid for. Nobody under thirty believes half of the tale of Magnus.
- Old Burnt Driver bounty notices still hang behind some fort bars. The Burnt Drivers' lieutenants carried a ring of burn at the neck.

## Blackwell

- The refinery town at the road's south end. Every truck on the road burns its diesel, and Blackwell pays in fuel, never in coin. It buys whoever is for sale.
- Its wells are failing, and the fourth came up black sludge the season before the run. The covenant, its water deal with Northgate, has run every season for twenty years on Blackwell's terms.
- Oilmaster Kasimir Vell ran it through the Magnus years. He died in the gap, and his daughter Hedda Vell, the Oilmistress, 41 at the run, runs it now. She never lets a debt go unpaid, in either direction.
- Refinery runoff poisons the ground south of the town. The cheap water Blackwell sells the poor makes children sick.

## The Yards and the Wayfarers

- The Yards are an old truck stop fifteen kilometres south of Blackwell. About eighty salvagers live there, the Wayfarers, led by Sara, 59.
- The refinery's runoff has poisoned their ground and well. Their stored clean water was meant for eighty, and the sick families who came to their gate cut it to about six months.
- Salvage crews from the Yards strip wrecks on the north road and talk about their old hauler wherever they go.

## The black-water sickness

- A fever of the poisoned ground near the refinery. Children get it first, and it does not break on its own. Most of the south has never seen it cured.

## Northgate

- A shut valley with a pure mountain spring at the road's north end. Few southerners have reached it, its gates shut to anyone who comes up the road, and its winters kill the careless.
- Since twenty years ago its water has gone south in Blackwell tankers under the covenant. Northgate failed the bargain and gave too much at the gate.
- Warden Lio Marten, 79, runs it. His mother Ilse was the valley's doctor for forty years. Wren Sallow, 46, Blackwell-born, is his clerk and counsel, who counts every barrel twice.
- The game keeps Northgate beyond the last fort anyone has counted. The hauler never arrives, and no scene shows the gate. Its answers come down the relay.

## The cure rumor

- The road says Ala's trailer carries "the cure" for the black-water sickness. The bar talk began on the hiring night, and every driver on the road has heard it. It is wrong in its shape and right in its heart, as [the truths](#hidden-truths) say.

## People of the run

- Ala, 34, drives the mammoth hauler. She pays by the stretch and takes no questions. She counts the money out loud before she pays. Drive +3, Will +2. Her father Jon was a linked tanker driver, killed sixteen years ago. Her mother is Sara. Her dead sister's girl Maya, 12, lives at the Yards.
- Geezer, 54, is the linked driver of a small scout, hired in a fort bar. He has deep link scars at the neck and old bullet scars, and he says only that he once rode with Magnus. He checks every tire before he speaks. He took the hardest jobs for two years. The last one ended in a mine, and a salvage crew from the Yards gave him a ride south.
- Tibor, the barman of the Keepers' southern outpost, sells men like parts.
- Vale of the Strays, in her forties, leads a crew of hunters for hire. She names the price and the threat in one breath.
- Sara, Ala's mother. She is heard only in relay slips.

## The run

- Ala drives the hauler north and pays Geezer by the stretch. In the game, Geezer is the player's gun truck, and the hauler is the player's second truck. The one wallet holds the pay, and Geezer keeps both trucks fitted out of it. Ala asks no question about the money.
- A bounty rides on her truck. Hedda set it in diesel, paid at Blackwell, when the Keepers sent the hauler north under Northgate's summons. Every scavenger, merc and out-of-work bounty man on the road heard it. That is why the groups never stop.
- By the eighth stretch the bounty pays for the crates burned. Hedda's soldiers refuse to fire on the sick at the Yards, she shuts Blackwell's gates and sends a confession to every fort, and she never calls the bounty off. The crews in the badlands are owed diesel by nobody, and they keep coming.
- Two hundred people walk north behind the hauler, strong enough to go, with no escort of their own. Maya is among them. By the twelfth stretch the relay carries that news and Northgate's answer.

## Hidden truths

Spoiler note: scenes before the twelfth stretch must not state T1, and must not say Geezer was at the pass.

- T1. Magnus lives as Geezer. Nobody alive knows. Ala learns by the twelfth stretch only that he was a Burnt Driver. The player sees the name in the twelfth stretch flashback and may confess it.
- T2. The abandoned army column at the tank line was a link regiment. Each seat is wired to one driver's nerves and burns the next driver who takes it. Magnus's worst neck scars come from forcing a seat that was not his.
- T3. The burned body brought in as Magnus was not him. He tore the plug out of the burning seat, which left the deep ring scar at the base of his skull, and walked into the badlands. He drove other people's trucks for hire as Geezer and never went back for anyone.
- T4. The exile Magnus sent away for talking of the bounty led Blackwell's hunters to the tank line and sold the body. He was killed two years later collecting another bounty, and with him the last Burnt Driver who knew Magnus's face died.
- T5. The trailer holds the journals of Doctor Ilse Marten, forty years of a valley doctor's notes. They show that southern children with the black-water sickness got well on the spring within a season. Northgate's elders hid this so the south would not come up the road. Keeper rider Piet Arno carried them south, died of fever at the Yards, and Sara hid them for three years. Ala carries them to trade for a place in the valley.
- T6. The cure is the spring, and the journals are the proof. The crates hold no cure. Read aloud, the journals end the covenant, and every poisoned family walks north. That is the background and never the story.
- T7. Maya, 12, drank the cheap black water Blackwell sold the Yards this spring. Her fever breaks only on the valley's water. If Ala turns back, Maya dies waiting.
- T8. Ala's father Jon died in Blackwell's first water convoy, taken at the pass by the Burnt Drivers with no survivors. Magnus led that ambush. Geezer knows it from stretch 1 on. Ala believes her father died a hero running water to Northgate. The convoy was carrying Northgate water south for Blackwell.
- T9. Hedda wants the journals burned, because proof read aloud ends the covenant. The bounty asks for the crates burned, and the hauler with them.
- T10. Ala learns at the twelfth stretch that her father died to the Burnt Drivers. She does not learn that Geezer was Magnus or led the pass, unless the player says so.
- T11. The bounty has nobody left to pay it, and the crews keep coming.

Answers the play gave:

- Who keeps the forts: the Keepers, under Dane.
- What Ala carries and why the road wants it: the doctor's journals, and the road believes it is the cure.
- Who Ala does it for: Maya, and the Yards, and her father.
- Who set the bounty and why the hunt does not stop: Hedda Vell, and a bounty with nobody left to pay it.
- What the road learns of the people behind: the Warden will open the gate for the walkers, the Keepers feed them, Vale holds the road behind them, and Maya rides among them.
- Magnus's gang: the Burnt Drivers, and how they ended.

Game master adaptations:

- The story game put Northgate's gate after the twelfth stretch. The run is endless, so canon keeps Northgate beyond the last fort anyone has counted and gives the payoff by relay.
- Ala pays Geezer by the stretch and he keeps both trucks fitted from that pay, which matches the one wallet.
- Scene openings state only what every run shares: hunters came and both trucks got through.
