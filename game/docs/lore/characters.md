# Characters

Named people of Icarus. Each one has a main way of coping from [Theme](../theme.md), a flaw that comes from it, and a voice. Their groups are in [Peoples](peoples.md). J.J. of Waste Of Time Radio is in [Lore](../lore.md).

## Alan Graves

Way: control.

The President of Nose has led its Central Committee for 30 years. He believes the basin will die without rationing and discipline. He is probably right about that. He can no longer tell the good of Nose from his own hold on it.

## Captain Silver

Way: control.

Silver commands the Nose Army post at the Salvage Yard. He was sent there as a demotion, and he means to earn his way back by bringing the Yard under Nose. In public he is a tough and fair officer. In private he is a cruel despot.

He believes the only future for the Yard is to belong to Nose. He is willing to stage a disaster to prove it.

## The Administrator

Way: fear.

The Administrator runs the Salvage Yard with his gang. The Yard is free, and he wants to keep it free. That means getting the Nose post out. He is a crook. He also cares for his people as well as he can.

His line is "I care for my people as I can."

## Ain "Buffalo" Christopher

Way: oppose.

Ain is the unacknowledged son of a Nose committee member. His child died. After that he fled Nose and started his own gang. Now he wants to be king of the raider camps, whatever it costs. He believes the end justifies the means, and nobody knows the whole of his past.

His line is "I will be king of the camps, and I will pay any price for it."

## Kaplan

Way: embrace.

Kaplan is Ain's right hand and a road veteran. He cares about his people more than about Ain's crown. He misses the old days, when the gang was a pack and not an army.

His lines are "It was better when we were just a pack" and "The dead don't care about glory."

## Fane

Way: control.

Fane is an old trinket trader in the Nose market. He buys relics from the ship and anything else nobody else understands. People call him the soul lender. He will lend against anything, and he always collects. He is a cunning businessman with no principles, and he trades in information as much as in goods.

## The Preacher

Way: fear.

The Preacher leads the inner circle of the Sunward. He wrote their teachings about the thing in the crater, and he believes every word. He has never been as close to the green glow as he tells his followers.

## The Apprentice

Way: control.

The Apprentice was a member of the Lunar Society until it cast him out. He found implants in the ship's wreck and put a blue eye in his own head. Through it he sees marks on the wreck that nobody else can see. He has filled his body with every implant he could find, and he is over 150 years old.

He believes the people of the basin are slowly dying out and that only a new start can save humanity. He has not told anyone what that new start would be.

He speaks in sermons. One of them goes like this:

> Do you fear insanity? The world we know is branches upon branches of an old tree. The tree is reason itself, and the branches are the ways of reasoning. The tree is dead. People are like ants, eating it from the inside. To find a new path we must start from the roots and face insanity if we must.
