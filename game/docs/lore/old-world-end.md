# The end of the Old World

How the Old World ended, first as it happened and then as people tell it today. Everything here answers to [Theme](../theme.md). The game never tells this history straight. It shows only the myth, the words people use and the signs.

## Where the Old World splits from ours

The Old World's history is our own until about the time LCD screens came in. After that it branches off.

## What happened

### The Spiral

The Spiral was not one war, epidemic or drought. It was many disasters at once, and they overlapped by bad luck. Together they made the world unstable and short of everything.

Under that pressure some powerful groups traded the long term for short-term gain. They broke their word and took from their neighbors to get through one more day. They did it to stay powerful and rich, not to stay alive.

When one side cheated, its rivals had to cheat too or go under. Each round of cheating made the next disaster worse, so the cycle fed itself.

### The Cry

The Cry is Gulke's Conjecture. Gulke was an influential geoscientist. When the Spiral was already far along, he claimed that Earth might have only 10 to 30 years left to give birth to a civilization that could travel in space. After that would come only a slow fall into nothing. The claim was about climate and resources.

It was only a hypothesis, but it became popular. One superpower took it up, and then the others had to take it up too. Soon too many military contracts depended on it for anyone to question it again.

### The Race

Earth was dying, so superpowers and corporations raced to leave it. They built ships at any cost. Technology moved fast, but only in the narrow fields the ships needed.

The Race made most people poor. Workers lit their homes with diesel generators and then went to build a colony ship from 8 to 9, six days a week. It was an arms race and a race to the bottom. Climate, land and human rights all went into the furnace. Every side feared that the others would use up the last resources first and keep it on the ground forever.

### The Exodus

The Exodus was the launch of the ships. Everyone powerful enough to leave left, or tried to. The powerful who stayed were of two kinds:

- People who were outplayed and forced to stay.
- A rare kind of psychopath who wanted to rule a dying world.

### The Chaos

The Exodus left empty places where power had been. The people left behind were afraid that they were abandoned for good. Together the empty places and the fear turned into the Chaos. For about 200 years the world was made again, mostly in fire and blood. Some people also call it the Endless Age or the Crucible.

The Chaos ended about 50 years ago. [World facts](world.md) and [Timeline](timeline.md) hold the dates.

### The Forsaken

The Forsaken is the present world. The word means left behind.

## How people tell it

Nobody alive saw the Old World end. The story came down through the generations and became a myth. People tell it as six words, always in this order: Spiral. Cry. Race. Exodus. Chaos. Forsaken.

Each word is a stage. The facts behind the words are mostly lost, and each people fills them in its own way.

- The Spiral stays deep in everyone's mind. To spiral means to go mad, or to fall out of order, as a town or a crew can.
- Tribal and unlettered people link the Spiral to the sun, because the sun is hot and burns them.
- Some groups believe the Race was a real race between gods on machines, and that the race gave birth to the world.
- The Exodus has mixed with the old religions. People often tell it as the moment the worthy rose to heaven, or the moment judgment sent people to hell.

## The signs

Four stages have a sign: Spiral, Race, Exodus and Chaos. The Cry and the Forsaken have none.

- Spiral is a spiral with three arms.
- Race is one spike that slants up to the right, thick at the bottom and thin as a needle at the top.
- Exodus is an X cut as four rays that point outward from the middle.
- Chaos is an O.

The signs fit one grid, like the letters of a small font. Drawn over each other in their order, they make one combined sign. The spike pierces the spiral, and the rays cross the O and stick out past it. People cut the signs into metal and stone with knives, so every stroke is a straight, sharp cut that tapers to a point. Only the O is round. The game draws it on the loading screen, where it builds up one stage at a time, and paints the signs on cliffs and props.
