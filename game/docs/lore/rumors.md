# Rumors

Things people in Icarus say. Some are true, some are half true and some are false. A rumor is evidence, never an answer, as [Theme](../theme.md) describes.
## What the ship was

Nobody agrees, and each guess has its believers.

- The ship was a launch platform, and the basin was built to catch its falling stages.
- The ship is a power plant, and the reactor in the Fallen Sun was its heart, not its engine.
- The ship came from a colony on another world, and its people came home to die.
- The basin is purgatory, or Hell, and the ship is how the damned arrived.
- The ship was built to carry people away from a dying Earth, and it never got out.

## The sky

- Astronomers of the old Alpha Tower Observatory tried to work out where the ship came from by the movement of the stars.
- The Observatory found something else coming toward the basin, and kept it secret.
- One night two moons of different colors stood over the basin.
- A philosopher lives alone in the Alpha Tower. He is the smartest person in Icarus.

## The wreck and its tech

- The ship's control room was destroyed in the war between Nose and Bowl.
- Nose soldiers were locked inside the control room during the war, and nobody opened it again.
- Before the fall, people had machines that moved memories from one person to another.
- Somewhere under the Fallen Sun lies a warhead big enough to end the whole basin.
- Nose has the secret of a weapon that goes through any armor, and it guards the secret closely.
- Some drivers who sleep near the green glow wake up knowing things they never learned.

## Powers and money

- Pierce Corporation controls both Nose and Bowl through what their rulers owe it.
- Pierce insures salvage runs like a casino. It bets that the truck comes back.
- The Old Army wanted to bury the Fallen Sun under a great bomb, so that nobody could fight over it again.
- The Old Army did not run out of food. It was starved out on purpose.
- Pierce sold the Old Army's guns to Scrapjaw, then made every driver buy a gun to be insured.
- Somebody in the Pierce office sold the roads of insured trucks to robbers. He went west and never came back.
- The Nose Committee rents its workshop line with Pierce money.
- The Administrator keeps a book in the crane office that could bring down both towns.
- The old Senate families on the top terraces send a tanker to Dustwell that never shows up in any book.

## The road

- A steel truck drives the wreck fields at night. It cannot be stopped, and nobody has survived meeting it.
- The Lady of the Road is a voice on the radio that warns drivers of danger ahead.
- One day the basin will dry out completely, and everyone in it will die of thirst.
- A Nose swivel has one pin, and on a sharp bend it will swing the gun into your cab.
- In the west there are towns whose trucks carry no guns, because a guard paid by every town keeps the roads.
- The glass on the Glass Flats all points the same way, toward the Fallen Sun.
- The number the Sunward chant at the rim is the year the radio count stops.
- Kiln riders let you go if you fight hard enough. They strip you if you don't.
- The Sunward take strangers into the crater, and the strangers do not come back.
- Some people out past the glass live without towns and without trucks, and nobody knows how.
- A crew once camped in the wreck and their sentry fell asleep. In the morning the sentry was gone, and his boots were still standing by the fire.
