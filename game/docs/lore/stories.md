# Stories

Seeds for events and jobs in Icarus. None of them is built in the game yet. A story never has a right side. The player can join any side, play both, or walk away, and the world goes on without them. Groups are in [Peoples](peoples.md) and people in [Characters](characters.md).

## The free yard

The Salvage Yard is free. The Administrator's gang runs it, and a Nose Army post under Captain Silver sits inside it. Each side waits for the other to slip.

1. Raiders start hitting the Yard's trucks. Silver blames the Administrator for weak defense and says only Nose can protect the Yard.
2. The Administrator asks the player to find out how the raiders always know which trucks to hit.
3. A careful player finds that a Nose courier sold the routes to the raiders. A careless one learns only that someone sold them.
4. A Bowl agent approaches the player quietly. Bowl wants to stop Nose taking the Yard, and it will watch what the player does.
5. Each side asks the player for help. The Administrator wants the Nose post burned. Silver wants the Yard gate left open one night, so raiders get in and Nose can save the Yard.
6. The player can help one side, sell both, or bring the two sides together against the raiders.

The ending follows the choice. If one side wins, the raiders are driven off at a high cost and the Yard changes hands or stays free. If both sides work together, the raiders are driven off cheaply. If neither does anything, the raiders take the Yard for a while.

## The world moves

Every few days each major group makes one move of its own, whatever the player does. J.J. reports the moves that reach the radio. These moves are examples of the kind:

- Pierce sends a salvage crew deep into the Fallen Sun, and it does not come back.
- Nose sends its secret police to look for Bowl agents in the Salvage Yard.
- Nose occupies a pump or a crossroads that Bowl counts as its own, and the two towns move closer to war.
- Bowl offers free drivers pay to fight raiders beside its patrols.
- The Guild raises its prices after a courier is robbed.
- A town runs short of food or water, and the price climbs across the basin.

## The relic

1. The player finds a strange part in a wreck that no trader can name.
2. Fane offers a good price for it. Someone steals it before the deal closes.
3. The thieves were hired by Fane himself, or by the Apprentice, or by both.
4. Following the part leads to the last member of the Lunar Society, who knows what it is.
5. The Sunward also want the part, and they take the old scholar before the player arrives.

## The Old Army

The Old Army's trucks still stand where they stopped at Old Orchard. Their logs, orders and cargo hold pieces of why it left. Some pieces say it ran out of food. Others say someone starved it out on purpose. The player can put the pieces together, and the answer changes how Nose and Bowl look.
