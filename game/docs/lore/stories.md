# Stories

Seeds for events and jobs in Icarus. None of them is built in the game yet. A story never has a right side. The player can join any side, play both, or walk away, and the world goes on without them. Groups are in [Peoples](peoples.md) and people in [Characters](characters.md).

## The free yard

The Salvage Yard is free. The Administrator's gang runs it, and a Nose Army post under Captain Silver sits inside it. Each side waits for the other to slip.

1. Raiders start hitting the Yard's trucks. Silver blames the Administrator for weak defense and says only Nose can protect the Yard.
2. The Administrator asks the player to find out how the raiders always know which trucks to hit.
3. A careful player finds that a Nose courier sold the routes to the raiders. A careless one learns only that someone sold them.
4. A Bowl agent approaches the player quietly. Bowl wants to stop Nose taking the Yard, and it will watch what the player does.
5. Each side asks the player for help. The Administrator wants the Nose post burned. Silver wants the Yard gate left open one night, so raiders get in and Nose can save the Yard.
6. The player can help one side, sell both, or bring the two sides together against the raiders.

The ending follows the choice. If one side wins, the raiders are driven off at a high cost and the Yard changes hands or stays free. If both sides work together, the raiders are driven off cheaply. If neither does anything, the raiders take the Yard for a while.

## The world moves

Every few days each major group makes one move of its own, whatever the player does. J.J. reports the moves that reach the radio. These moves are examples of the kind:

- Pierce sends a salvage crew deep into the Fallen Sun, and it does not come back.
- Nose sends its secret police to look for Bowl agents in the Salvage Yard.
- Nose occupies a pump or a crossroads that Bowl counts as its own, and the two towns move closer to war.
- Bowl offers free drivers pay to fight raiders beside its patrols.
- The Guild raises its prices after a courier is robbed.
- A town runs short of food or water, and the price climbs across the basin.

## The relic

1. The player finds a strange part in a wreck that no trader can name.
2. Fane offers a good price for it. Someone steals it before the deal closes.
3. The thieves were hired by Fane himself, or by the Apprentice, or by both.
4. Following the part leads to the last member of the Lunar Society, who knows what it is.
5. The Sunward also want the part, and they take the old scholar before the player arrives.

## The Old Army

The Old Army's trucks still stand where they stopped at Old Orchard. Their logs, orders and cargo hold pieces of why it left. Some pieces say it ran out of food. Others say someone starved it out on purpose. The player can put the pieces together, and the answer changes how Nose and Bowl look.

## The Archon's truck

1. Agnes Ondra at Dustwell asks the player to carry a sealed copy of her pump book to Mirela Duarte in Bowl.
2. The book shows water going missing every week into the household truck's barrels, whose loads a Senate order keeps out of the book. Mirela Duarte's Canal book proves the rest of Agnes's numbers are real.
3. Tomas Duarte, Mirela's cousin in a Bowl coat, wrote a patrol report about the household truck. It sits on the Archon's desk.
4. The Archon's household offers the player good money to lose the copy on the road.
5. The player can deliver the copy, sell it, read it out in the Bowl market, or drop it in a ditch.

## The north bend

Ash Kerimov sits alone at the north bend where Emil Kessel died. He stops every truck and asks to see its brake rod. A driver with a cheap Nose rod gets a choice: buy a proper one at the Salvage Yard, or drive on. Kiln Camp wants him gone, because he slows the road. Captain Silver wants him gone too, because he talks to Nose drivers. Both offer the player a job.

## Emil's brake rod

1. Mara Kessel, an officer in Captain Silver's post, quietly asks the player to bring her the broken brake rod from Emil Kessel's wreck on the north road.
2. The wreck has already been stripped. The rod turns up at the Salvage Yard or on a Kiln truck.
3. The rod proves that the Nose gate issued it as cheap steel. Silver wants it destroyed. The Administrator wants it kept, along with what it proves.
4. Mara has her own pact with the Administrator. The player decides who learns about it.

## The Reading of Names

Once a year Lusa Fenwick reads the names of the dead on the Canyon Bridge deck: Hal Sorensen, Jens Vosk, Brother Anselm, Wendell Ondra, Emil Kessel. Drivers park on the bare deck and stand bareheaded while she reads. Someone in the crowd killed one of the people named. A player who stays for the whole reading hears who, from a man who drinks too much afterward.

## The last courier

Tamsin Roe is the last Guild courier with no gun. Vesna Hale rides behind her with the only rifle. This year Vesna is sick. The Guild asks the player to ride as Tamsin's pair on the south loop. Tamsin will not take a gun. Kiln riders know her truck and let it pass, but this season someone new rides for Kiln.

## The hook house

Green Pit's pumps draw mud every dry year. A Nose soldier from Silver's post sits by the hooks and watches who hangs what. Hadiza Okon asks the player to find out what he reports and to whom. If the hooks stop meaning safety, the last place where Kiln riders and haulers drink together goes with them.

## The arrival

The game opens with the player walking into the basin from outside. Nobody ever says who they are or why they came. Their canteen is empty and the heat is closing in.

1. On foot at the basin's edge, they walk toward a water tower on an abandoned farm.
2. An old pickup sits in a collapsed shed under the tower. It starts on the third try and coughs.
3. They learn to drive on open dirt toward a wreck in sight.
4. They search the wreck and patch the engine so it stops misfiring.
5. One raider truck takes them on the road out. The pickup is weak, so a loss is likely but not forced.
6. On a loss, the first fall plays: the barrel lowers and the driver lives. They wake in Bowl on Hattie's bench at the cistern house. A stranger towed the truck in and left no name, and the yard by the gate sells what the raider took.
